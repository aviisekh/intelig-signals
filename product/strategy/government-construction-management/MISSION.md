# Mission

Government construction teams still track site progress, labor attendance, and material movement on paper. Engineers are responsible for many projects at once, but the information they need is fragmented across notebooks, phone calls, photos, and contractor updates.

This product exists to give government engineers, contractor companies, and site supervisors a shared operational system for construction project execution. The first version focuses on digitizing daily site records so engineers can see what changed today, what is blocked, and whether each contractor is progressing against the approved plan.

## Problem

- Government engineers oversee multiple construction sites with limited real-time visibility.
- Site supervisors record daily labor, materials, and progress manually.
- Contractor reporting is difficult to audit because evidence is spread across paper logs and informal messages.
- Material logistics and labor attendance are easy to lose, duplicate, or manipulate.
- Project decisions lack a reliable historical record.

## Mission Outcomes

- Replace paper-based daily site tracking with structured mobile-first workflows.
- Give engineers a live dashboard for assigned projects and sites.
- Create an auditable record of supervisor submissions, labor attendance, material receipts, and progress evidence.
- Establish a clean database foundation before feature coding, as decided in the 2026-05-26 pivot meeting.

## Non-Goals

- This is not the laundry or cleaning-service marketplace discussed earlier.
- This is not a generic task manager or Jira replacement.
- This is not a full government procurement platform in the MVP.
